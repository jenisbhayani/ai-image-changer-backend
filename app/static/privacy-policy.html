Privacy Policy

Application: AI Image Changer (com.jdbhayani9.aiimagechanger)
Effective Date: October 25, 2026 | Last Updated: October 25, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to AI Image Changer ("we", "our", or "the App"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy outlines our data handling practices in accordance with the Google Play Developer Program Policies, GDPR, and CCPA.

2. Data Collection and Usage

A. Photo and Image Processing

  • On-Device Processing: Manual cropping, rotating, flipping, adjustments, filters, and offline background removals occur 100% locally on your device. No photo data leaves your phone.
  • Cloud AI Processing: When you use AI HD background removal or Passport face analysis, the image is securely transmitted via HTTPS to our dedicated processing server.
  • Ephemeral Storage: Uploaded images are processed in-memory or transient cache and are automatically deleted within 1 hour. We do not permanently store, archive, or catalog user photos.
  • Facial Analysis in Passport Studio: Face detection is executed on-the-fly to calculate head coverage ratios and posture tilt for official passport guidelines. We do not perform biometric identity verification or create facial recognition profiles.

B. Local Device Storage

The app stores recent edit thumbnails and recipe settings on your device's internal storage so you can easily resume editing. You can clear this data at any time via Settings → Clear local app data.

C. App Permissions

  • Camera: To take photos for editing.
  • Photo Library: To select photos from your gallery and save edited results.

3. Third-Party Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID) and usage data to serve ads and prevent ad fraud. You can manage personalized ad settings in your device settings under Google → Ads. For more information, visit Google Privacy & Terms.

4. Data Security

All communication between the App and backend servers uses Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS). We do not sell or share your personal photographs with any third parties.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is a general-utility photo editor and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You can clear all local workspace items and edit history at any time using the in-app Delete local app data button in the Settings tab.
